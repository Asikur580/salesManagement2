# Reports & Analytics Module - User Manual

The Reports & Analytics module provides a powerful way to track your business performance, revenue trends, and profitability. This guide will help you understand and utilize all the features of the analytics dashboard.

---

## 1. Dashboard Widgets
Your main Dashboard now includes real-time business metrics for quick decision-making.

- **Total Monthly Revenue**: The total value of all orders placed in the current month.
- **Monthly Profit**: Estimated net profit (Sales Price minus Product Cost Price) for the current month.
- **Pending Orders**: A count of all orders that are currently in 'Pending' status and require attention.
- **Low Stock Items**: Alerts for products that have fallen below their set safety stock levels.

---

## 2. Navigating to Reports
You can access advanced analytics through the Sidebar.

**Steps:**
1. Open the **Sidebar Menu**.
2. Locate and click on the **"Reports"** category.
3. Click on **"Sales Reports"** to open the main analytics dashboard.

---

## 3. Using Date Range Filters
By default, the report shows the last 30 days of data. You can customize this to see performance for any period (e.g., today, last week, or an entire year).

**How to filter:**
1. Use the **Start Date** picker to choose the beginning of the period.
2. Use the **End Date** picker to choose the conclusion of the period.
3. Click the **"Filter"** button to refresh all charts, widgets, and tables with data from your selected period.

---

## 4. Understanding the Analytics

### Sales Trend (Area Chart)
This chart visualizes your daily revenue. It helps you identify high-performing days (peaks) and slower periods, allowing you to plan promotions or staffing accordingly.

### Source Comparison (Online vs POS)
The Pie Chart breaks down where your sales are coming from:
- **ONLINE**: Orders placed through the web shop.
- **POS (Offline)**: Orders processed via the Point of Sale system in-store.

### Profit & Margin
The **Net Profit** card shows the actual money made after subtracting the cost of goods sold (COGS). This is essential for understanding the health of your business beyond just revenue.

### Top Selling Products
This table lists your best-performing items. Use this to determine which products are your "Best Sellers" and ensure they are always in stock.

---

## 5. Exporting Reports
You can download your data for external sharing, accounting, or further analysis.

**Export Options:**
1. **PDF Export**: Generates a professional, print-ready document containing the summary widgets, sales tables, and source breakdowns. Ideal for monthly meetings or archiving.
2. **CSV Export**: Downloads a detailed spreadsheet (`.csv` file) of all individual orders in the selected period. You can open this file in **Microsoft Excel** or **Google Sheets** for custom calculations.

---

## 6. Access Control
Access to the Reports & Analytics module is limited based on user roles. 
- **Admins & Managers**: Have full access to view and export reports.
- **Standard Staff**: May not see the Reports menu depending on their specific permission settings to ensure financial data privacy.
